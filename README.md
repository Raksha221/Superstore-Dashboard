Sales & Business Analytics Dashboard

An interactive Tableau project built to explore sales performance,
profitability, product trends, and business metrics using the Sample
Superstore dataset. The workbook includes KPI views, trend analysis,
category and regional breakdowns, and interactive controls for exploring
performance from different perspectives.

Project Objectives

*Monitor key business metrics such as sales, profit, profit margin,
order count, and return rate.
*Compare performance across regions, categories, sub-categories, and
time periods.
*Identify top-performing products and areas that may need attention.
*Enable interactive analysis through parameters and filters.

Tools & Technologies

Tableau --- dashboards, visualizations, calculated fields,
parameters, and interactivity

Sample Superstore dataset --- sales, orders, products,
customers, and regional performance data

Dashboard Features

*KPI views: Sales, profit margin, and return-rate metrics.
*Sales trends: Monthly sales and profit trend visualizations.
*Category analysis: Sales and profit comparisons by category and
sub-category.
*Regional analysis: Sales performance by state and region.
*Product analysis: Top-five product view.
*Dynamic metric selection: Switch the metric displayed between
Sales, Profit, and Order Count.
*Dynamic grouping: View results by Region, Category, or
Sub-Category.
*Interactive exploration: Use available dashboard controls and
filters to investigate performance.
